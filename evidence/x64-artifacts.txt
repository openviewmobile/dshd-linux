## Flatpak x64

The flatpak target was attempted with a privileged container and a Flathub remote.
`org.freedesktop.Sdk/x86_64/24.08` was installed successfully, but the build then
needed the 24.08 Platform runtime; downloading it stalled at ~40 MB in the current
network, so the build was stopped. x64 Flatpak still needs a pre-seeded Platform
runtime or a faster Flathub mirror. Arm64 Flatpak remains the verified Flatpak artifact.
