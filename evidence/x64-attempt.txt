## Resolution

Added `0003-primary-runtime-mirrors.patch`: Node defaults to
`https://npmmirror.com/mirrors/node`, Python standalone defaults to
`https://registry.npmmirror.com/-/binary/python-build-standalone` (both overridable by
`DSH_NODE_DIST_MIRROR` / `DSH_PYTHON_RELEASE_MIRROR`). With those mirrors, x64
AppImage/deb/rpm were produced and the deb was launched under Xvfb; see
[x64-artifacts.txt](x64-artifacts.txt). Flatpak still needs the Flathub runtime.
